# EcoInference Trademark Policy

The **code** in this repository is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
The **name and brand** are not. This document explains the difference and what you may do
with each.

This split is intentional and standard practice for open-source applications. The license
gives you broad freedom with the software. The trademark exists so that when someone
installs something called "EcoInference," they know where it came from — which protects
users more than it protects us.

## Marks covered

- **EcoInference**™ (word mark)
- The EcoInference logo and app icon
- **ecoinference.ai** and associated domains

These are claimed as common-law marks. They are not yet registered, so the ™ symbol is
used rather than ®.

## What you may do without asking

**Use the software.** Run it, study it, modify it, distribute it, build on it — under the
terms of the MIT license, for any purpose including commercial ones. The trademark doesn't
restrict that, and it is not a back door to restricting it.

**Refer to the project by name.** You can say your work is "based on EcoInference," "a fork
of EcoInference," "compatible with EcoInference," or write articles, tutorials, reviews, and
academic papers about it. Accurate, descriptive reference is fair use and always permitted.

**Redistribute unmodified builds** under the EcoInference name, as long as they're genuinely
unmodified and clearly sourced from this project.

**Use the logo** when linking to or writing about the project, provided it isn't altered and
isn't used in a way implying we endorse or sponsor you.

## What needs permission

**Naming a modified version "EcoInference."** If you change the code, please call it
something else. You may still say it's derived from EcoInference — just don't ship it under
our name. This is the core of the policy, and it's the *only* thing asked of a forker.

**Publishing to an app store under the EcoInference name**, or anything close enough to
confuse users.

**Registering domains, social handles, or package names** containing "EcoInference."

**Using the name in your company, product, or service name**, or in a way that suggests an
official relationship, partnership, or endorsement that doesn't exist.

**Merchandise** carrying the marks.

## Why fork naming matters

If a modified build ships under our name, users can't tell whose software they're actually
running. That matters more than usual for an app like this: it handles personal
conversations, holds an API key, can execute code on the device, and can access location and
messaging. A user's judgement about whether to trust it depends on knowing its true source.

Renaming your fork isn't a restriction on your freedom to fork. It's what makes the fork
honest.

## Requesting permission

Email **info@ecoinference.ai** describing what you'd like to do. Reasonable requests are
generally granted, particularly for community, educational, and non-commercial uses.

## Note for forkers

Forking doesn't transfer any trademark rights to you, and it doesn't take any away from you
either — your own name and branding remain entirely yours. See [FORKING.md](FORKING.md) to get
started; renaming is the only thing this policy asks of you.
